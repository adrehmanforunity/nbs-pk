About the company

National Business Services

National Business Services (NBS) is the company behind OmniCX, an enterprise customer experience software platform for connected service, sales, support, and customer operations.

What NBS offers

NBS presents OmniCX as its customer experience platform. It brings customer conversations, CRM and case workflows, operational visibility, automation, and AI assistance together. The platform is designed for organizations managing high-volume or complex customer operations.
