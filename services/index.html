OmniCX capabilities

Software capabilities for connected customer service.

NBS offers OmniCX, a customer experience software platform for organizations managing customer service, sales, support, retention, and back-office operations. The capabilities below describe the platform—not a separate outsourced contact-center service.

What OmniCX supports

Voice, video, social, and digital service

Coordinate calls, video support, social conversations, messaging, self-service, and assisted customer journeys.

CRM and case management

Bring customer records, issue history, profile updates, and internal workflows into the interaction context.

Sales and retention

Organize lead engagement, follow-up tasks, customer lifecycle activity, and campaign responses.

Quality and workforce intelligence

Review conversation quality, service levels, queue health, staffing performance, and operational trends.

Engagement automation

Coordinate notifications, outbound campaigns, routing logic, and workflow-driven communications.

AI assistance and integrations

Support knowledge guidance, summaries, conversation review, and handoffs to connected systems, subject to configuration.

Is OmniCX a managed contact-center service?

The information on this site describes OmniCX as a software platform. Ask NBS about product fit, available capabilities, integrations, and deployment options.
