Google Gems to Skills: 2026 deadlines and a business migration checklist

Google has published specific dates for moving from Gems to Skills. Personal-account users face the first deadline this month; Workspace accounts follow later. Here is what changes, which files may not transfer, and what teams can do now.

By National Business Services · Published 7 October 2026 · Updated 2 November 2026

Google describes a Gemini Skill as reusable instructions that can guide recurring tasks. Rather than re-entering the same preferences and steps in every conversation, a user can create a Skill, have Gemini apply it when relevant, or combine Skills for a more involved task. Google says Skills are intended to replace Gems as its way to tailor Gemini for specific tasks.

This is a product update, not evidence that every organization should move a process to generative AI. The business value depends on account availability, the task, data handling, controls, and human review.

What Google says is changing

Google's Gemini release notes describe Skills as reusable instructions that can be used in Gemini chats. Google's transition guidance says Gems will be automatically recreated as Skills, with different dates depending on account type.

  • Personal Google Accounts for users 18 and over: Gems become read-only on 17 November 2026 and are removed from Gemini Apps on 17 December 2026.
  • Workspace business, enterprise, and non-profit accounts: Google lists March 2027.
  • Workspace education accounts: Google lists June 2027.
  • Personal accounts for users under 18: Google says the transition will happen later and has not yet provided a date.

These are Google's stated timelines as checked on 2 November 2026. Workspace teams should confirm their account-specific schedule with their administrator and check Google's help page for changes.

Check what will and will not move

Google says it will move Gems and supported files to Skills automatically. However, each Skill has a total file limit of 100 MB; if a Gem is over that limit, Google says it will remove the largest files first until the Skill is under the limit.

  • Files Google says will not transfer: GitHub files, audio, video, older Microsoft Office formats (.doc, .xls, .ppt), and archive formats such as .zip, .tar, .7z, .rar, .gz, and .tgz.
  • Personal accounts: download any needed Gem files before 17 December 2026; after Gems are removed, those files will no longer be available in Gemini Apps.
  • Manual migration: Google advises copying the Gem details into a new Skill and downloading knowledge files individually before uploading them. Check the current instructions for the account type you use.

Skills are useful for repeatable guidance, not automatic business controls

Reusable instructions could help teams standardize recurring work such as drafting a first-pass meeting agenda, applying an approved writing format, or organizing a routine summary. A Skill is still an instruction set used by an AI assistant; it does not by itself validate facts, enforce company policy, guarantee consistent output, or replace access controls, testing, or human approval.

Google currently documents limitations. Skills do not yet work with some Gemini features, including Canvas and Deep Research; many default tools available in Gems are not yet supported either. Access and capabilities can vary by account. Check the current product documentation before designing a workflow around a particular feature.

Business migration checklist

  • Inventory Gems: record each Gem's owner, purpose, instructions, attached files, and the business process that depends on it.
  • Confirm the right deadline: separate personal accounts from Workspace business, non-profit, and education accounts; ask the Workspace administrator about rollout and controls.
  • Protect important files: check for unsupported formats and the 100 MB limit. Personal-account users should download needed files before 17 December 2026.
  • Rebuild and verify: after Skills are available for the account, check the automatically created Skill or manually recreate it using Google's instructions. Test representative prompts and edge cases against the Gem's expected behavior.
  • Keep safeguards: review data-handling requirements, limit sensitive inputs, retain human approval for consequential outputs, and define a fallback if the Skill is unavailable or wrong.
  • Communicate the change: tell users where their Skills are, what files or capabilities did not carry over, and who can help with issues.

Bottom line

Personal-account users have a near-term deadline: Gems become read-only on 17 November 2026 and are removed on 17 December. Workspace business accounts have a later published timeline, but should still inventory Gems and check files before rollout. The automatic transition is helpful, but teams should verify that important instructions, files, and workflows still work before relying on them.
